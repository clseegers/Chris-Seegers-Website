Who I Am

Child of God, Husband, Father, Leader.

Chris Seegers — Founder, Exceptional Companies. Tara is my best friend, wife, and partner in all of it.
"Business is ministry, fueled by sustainable cashflow."

I'm a self-proclaimed capitalist missionary. I don't set faith aside to run a business, and I don't set business aside to live out my faith. Designed right, they're the same project — not two things I'm holding in tension.

We built Exceptional Companies on that idea: People, Process, Profit — in that order, never the other way around. We are an innovation company. We create ideas and move them into finished products and services we think can change the world.

Alongside my partners, I've been part of teams that have raised and deployed more than $750 million in capital — across business acquisitions, energy, real estate, and operating companies.

Exceptional Business Advisors helps the owner build a highly valuable business and exit it well. The liquidity event is stewarded by their financial advisory — or by Exceptional Wealth & Family Office if they need a wealth advisor. And along the way, Exceptional Insurance protects what they've built.

It's not a holding company bolting unrelated businesses together. It's one relationship with an owner, served at every stage — sell, protect, invest, transition.

Every entity runs on the same system — Exceptional OS, the framework we use to build, run, and eventually hand off a business.
